## Decision

At this checkpoint the live loop was causally connected and exactly accounted,
but it was not a chemically or biologically scaled organism model. Wall-clock
acceleration must remain outside the authoritative equations. The material and
energy contradictions below remain unresolved; they are not permission to tune
the world until it looks successful.

The observed Unity clumps were partly caused by a coarse field footprint and
artificial top-hat food placement. Resolving the field to metre cells and removing
the patches removes those two artifacts. An intermediate attempt to make the
existing boolean Sense marker authorize perfect gradient access was also rejected:
that marker does not represent a receptor, cue transport, detection physics, or
neural processing. The final bounded design instead declares generic local food
state as the owner-approved approximation and does not pretend the Sense marker
implements an organ.

## Direct measured evidence

The live founder developed from the current donor genotype has:

- physical structural mass: `187.4483642578125 kg`;
- displaced volume: `0.253421813249588 m3`;
- developed intake area: `0.13744468986988068 m2`;
- tracked structure: `1,000 q = 1e-6 mol` of limiting nutrient; and
- initial reserve: `2 q`.

That tracked structure corresponds to only `3.95e-6 mol m-3` within the body,
less than the live water's configured total limiting-nutrient concentration of
`2.225e-5 mol m-3`. If the design's P-like nutrient is phosphorus, the whole
187.45 kg body contains only about `30.97 micrograms P`. Exact bookkeeping cannot
make that mapping chemically plausible.

The comparison is not an instruction to add food or shrink bodies to obtain
survival. It is direct evidence that physical form, environmental inventory, and
lifecycle currencies currently belong to incompatible scales. A discarded
taxon-specific tissue estimate did not solve that problem and is deliberately not
part of the retained implementation: any future approximation must consume the
represented developed form, material inventories, and local environment without a
privileged fish or vertebrate branch.

Before removal of the three top-hat blooms, the 120-second, 1-metre-grid CPU
diagnostic recorded:

- 1,200 exact 0.1-second intervals;
- 4,682 live-creature behavior intervals;
- 2,940 searching, 1,742 idle, zero seeking, and zero cruising intervals;
- zero sampled producer, zero feeding debit, and zero grazed cells;
- all eight founders dead by approximately 55.8--65.8 simulated seconds; and
- exact whole-world and producer-account closure.

This is a legitimate extinction under the current initial state, not evidence
that searching should be made more successful.

After replacing those blooms with the homogeneous idealization, a 30-second CPU
diagnostic recorded:

- `13,233 q` of producer feeding debit, exactly reconciled to the field;
- `1,026` locally grazed cells and a maximum single-cell debit of `49 q`;
- `3.8694%` removed from the initial stock in the cells that were touched;
- all eight founders alive, with final reserves from `6` to `28 q`; and
- exact whole-world and producer-account closure.

That run proves local depletion, not biological feeding calibration. It also
falsified the intermediate claim that the boolean Sense device supplied a
physical sensor: all marked bodies reacted to arbitrarily small one-quantum
concentration gradients without receptor area, chemical-cue transport,
integration time, or molecular/particle noise. A numerical threshold chosen only
to suppress that response would be another unsupported behavior knob.

With direct biomass-gradient sensing temporarily disabled, the subsequent
10-second diagnostic recorded zero seeking, zero sensor producer telemetry, `521`
searching and `279` idle intervals. Contact feeding remained independent: the
eight bodies debited `826 q` from `67` local cells, and both exact books closed.
That was an investigative counterfactual, not the final controller. The later
owner decision explicitly accepted generic local food state without specifying
the unresolved organ; the bounded proof report records that final boundary.

## Energy-scale contradiction

The design document describes the limiting nutrient as P-like and cites a
phytoplankton caloric anchor of `45 kJ gC-1` with Redfield `C:N:P = 106:16:1`.
If the stored mole is phosphorus-equivalent, those declared anchors imply:

```text
45,000 J gC-1 * 12.011 gC molC-1 * 106 molC molP-1
    = 57,292,470 J molP-1
    = 0.05729247 J per 1e-9 mol quantum
```

The live fixture's producer value is `111.111... J q-1`, about 1,939 times
larger. This calculation is not itself authorization to change the value:
source names use `N` while the prose says P-like, and a consumer reserve is not
compositionally identical to producer biomass. It proves that nutrient identity
and material composition must be resolved before energy calibration.

## Required model order

1. Keep one authoritative simulation clock. Expose requested simulated-seconds
   per wall-second as driver pacing and report achieved rate separately.
2. Name the limiting element and its units unambiguously. Preserve exact integer
   transfers, but stop treating a fixture quantum-to-body conversion as chemistry.
3. Separate structural limiting nutrient from stored chemical energy. Feeding,
   maintenance, birth, and death must close the appropriate matter and energy
   paths without one scalar impersonating both currencies.
4. Give unresolved lower-scale machinery an empirically scoped approximation whose
   inputs are developed form, material inventories, and local environment. Do not
   introduce a taxon-specific implementation branch.
5. Separate reserve substrate before converting oxygen use to joules, and
   represent actuator tissue before replacing ideal actuator efficiency.
6. Extend Intake morphology with the collector and digestion properties needed
   to determine encounter, capture, and assimilation. Until then, label those
   values provisional rather than tuning them to sustain a population.
7. Replace the interim homogeneous idealization with explicit environmental
   initial data or a reproducible field spin-up when an ecosystem is selected.
   Add horizontal transport only when its physical driver is represented.
8. Use multirate numerical integration and GPU execution to reach ecological
   horizons. Never accelerate lifespan, mutation, food growth, or any other
   biological equation to make the world watchable.
