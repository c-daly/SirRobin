## Solver: blocking findings

1. **Empty bodies still produce eager NaNs.**

For `M00=M02=M22=0`:

```text
tr = 0
reg = EPS_SPD * tr = 0
det_reg = (0+0)(0+0)-0 = 0
Δv_reg = (0/0, 0/0) = NaN
```

That happens before the outer `torch.where`. It directly contradicts “finite on every body” and “even for `M=0`, `det_reg=reg² > 0`” ([S0 spec:82](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:82), [S0 spec:97](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:97), [S0 spec:108](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:108)).

The outer selection may hide the output, but it does not make the eagerly evaluated branch finite.

2. **`det_safe` does not make the eigensystem finite at `M=0`.**

At `M=0`, `lam_max=0` and `lam_min=DET_FLOOR/0=∞` ([S0 spec:70](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:70), [S0 spec:71](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:71)). `kappa` happens to evaluate as `0/∞ = 0`, but `lam_min` is non-finite and the invalid body is misleadingly “well-conditioned” numerically. The claimed eager-finiteness guarantee is false.

3. **`κ` does not imply `det_safe == det`.**

The assertion at [S0 spec:106](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:106) is mathematically invalid because `κ` is scale-invariant while `DET_FLOOR` is absolute. For `M=αI`, `κ=1` for every positive `α`, but sufficiently small `α` gives `det=α²<DET_FLOOR`. The body selects `EXACT` while solving with the altered denominator `DET_FLOOR`, so `MΔv=P` need not close.

4. **`det_reg` is not proven safe in f32.**

Even for valid bodies, computing

```python
(M00+reg)*(M22+reg) - M02*M02
```

retains a cancellation path. “SPD in real arithmetic” does not prove that the computed f32 determinant is bounded away from zero. `EPS_SPD` and relevant input bounds are also not pinned in this document ([S0 spec:90](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:90), [S0 spec:98](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:98)).

5. **The corrected portions are correct.**

The dispatch uses only `κ>KAPPA_MAX` ([S0 spec:76](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:76), [S0 spec:86](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:86)). The regularization sign is right:

```text
(M+rI)Δv=P  ⇒  MΔv=P-rΔv=P+J_reg
J_reg=-rΔv
```

And the 2-D equation is now cleanly separated from vertical `J_c` ([S0 spec:113](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:113), [S0 spec:118](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:118), [S0 spec:120](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:120)).

It directly contradicts “finite on every body” and “even for `M=0`, `det_reg=reg² > 0`” ([S0 spec:82](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:82), [S0 spec:97](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:97), [S0 spec:108](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:108)).

The outer selection may hide the output, but it does not make the eagerly evaluated branch finite.

2. **`det_safe` does not make the eigensystem finite at `M=0`.**

At `M=0`, `lam_max=0` and `lam_min=DET_FLOOR/0=∞` ([S0 spec:70](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:70), [S0 spec:71](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:71)). `kappa` happens to evaluate as `0/∞ = 0`, but `lam_min` is non-finite and the invalid body is misleadingly “well-conditioned” numerically. The claimed eager-finiteness guarantee is false.

3. **`κ` does not imply `det_safe == det`.**

The assertion at [S0 spec:106](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:106) is mathematically invalid because `κ` is scale-invariant while `DET_FLOOR` is absolute. For `M=αI`, `κ=1` for every positive `α`, but sufficiently small `α` gives `det=α²<DET_FLOOR`. The body selects `EXACT` while solving with the altered denominator `DET_FLOOR`, so `MΔv=P` need not close.

4. **`det_reg` is not proven safe in f32.**

Even for valid bodies, computing

```python
(M00+reg)*(M22+reg) - M02*M02
```

retains a cancellation path. “SPD in real arithmetic” does not prove that the computed f32 determinant is bounded away from zero. `EPS_SPD` and relevant input bounds are also not pinned in this document ([S0 spec:90](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:90), [S0 spec:98](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:98)).

5. **The corrected portions are correct.**

The dispatch uses only `κ>KAPPA_MAX` ([S0 spec:76](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:76), [S0 spec:86](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:86)). The regularization sign is right:

```text
(M+rI)Δv=P  ⇒  MΔv=P-rΔv=P+J_reg
J_reg=-rΔv
```

And the 2-D equation is now cleanly separated from vertical `J_c` ([S0 spec:113](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:113), [S0 spec:118](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:118), [S0 spec:120](/mnt/c/Users/cddal/SirRobin/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-12-sirrobin-S0-kernel-spec.md:120)).
