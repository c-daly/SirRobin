# SirRobin S2 — canonical body and live locomotion implementation plan

**Date:** 2026-07-12<br>
**Status:** execution authority<br>
**Depends on:** S0 population-grounded GO; S1 conserved-nutrient GO<br>
**Authorizes:** S3 feeding, metabolism, and reproduction work only after the S2 decision report records GO

## 0. Executive position

S2 replaces hand-authored validation bodies with a single genome-derived body representation and replaces the
frozen-heading S0 experiment with persistent, yaw-integrating locomotion. It also establishes the additive-force
seam required by the sea-robin and land-transition destination. The live physics remains horizontal and aquatic:
hydrodynamics is the only nonzero contributor; gravity, buoyancy, contact, feeding, mutation, reproduction, and
creature energy reservoirs are not smuggled into this slice.

The archived S2 outline is useful but is not executable as written. It assumes the old 1,000-creature throughput
authority, an S1 energy reservoir that does not exist, a total coordinate convention contradicted by the landed
S0/S1 code, and a donor `StepLive` direction in which the selected "tail" is on the direction-of-travel side of
the centre of mass. This plan preserves the validated hydrodynamic equations and oracle evidence while correcting
those architectural and physical contradictions before implementation.

S2 is successful only if all of the following are true at once:

1. every production live body is `develop(genotype)`; S0 `BodyBatch.from_rows` remains fixture-only;
2. development is fixed-shape, deterministic on a fixed device, and independently corroborated;
3. the propulsive boundary is aft of the centre of mass in the declared forward direction;
4. yaw is generated by summed force moments and angular-momentum integration, not a heading or rate clamp;
5. numerical backstops, finite-state repairs, and solve regularization activate zero times in authorizing runs;
6. no `eff[]`, `swimProxy`, cached speed, defense score, or other capability scalar drives behavior;
7. the 5,000- and 10,000-creature H1/H2 live kernels clear their population-grounded real-time floors;
8. every claim is backed by a source-bound fixture, test, or telemetry artifact.

### 1.4 Explicitly superseded statements

- S2 does **not** debit metabolic energy. No reserve and heat reservoirs exist yet, so doing so would create a

- S2 does **not** store an angular velocity synchronized with angular momentum. `omega_yaw = L_yaw / I_yaw` is
  derived at the current pose.
- S2 does **not** expose a scalar `defense`, `agility`, `speed`, `swimProxy`, or generic `capabilities` cache.
  Future mechanisms query primitive geometry or execute physics.
- S2's performance floor is not the historical 90M number and not the archived 23.1M whole-tick estimate. The
  hard floor is one real-time 120 Hz step for every live creature: 600,000 creature-steps/s at 5,000 and
  1,200,000 at 10,000. Five- and ten-times-real-time are reported aspiration classes, not GO requirements.

## 2. Phase-0 reconciliations

No fixture is frozen and no production module is written until these decisions are reflected in the active docs.

### 2.1 Coordinate authority

The canonical public world frame is the already-landed S1 ENU frame:

- world `x`: east;
- world `y`: north;
- world `z`: up;
- surface `z=0`, water `z<0`;
- yaw is rotation about world `+z`.

The canonical public body frame is ROS FLU:

- body `+x`: forward;
- body `+y`: left;
- body `+z`: up.

The donor uses Unity storage `(right, up, longitudinal)` and horizontal XZ dynamics. Donor values are converted at
the fixture/genotype boundary by a named, tested orthogonal map. No donor axis convention may leak into public S2
contracts. S0 remains frozen in its donor-native frame and is not rewritten.

The adapter must pass basis, handedness, vector, quaternion-conjugation, semi-axis permutation, and round-trip
tests. A single declaration owns the map; inline swaps are forbidden.

### 2.2 Anatomical trailing-edge correction

The donor reconstruction chooses the largest `+z` reach as `tail`. Its one-shot S0 path points thrust from that
point toward the COM, while donor `StepLive` advances along local `+z`. Those directions are opposite. Porting the
live path literally would put the Lighthill boundary on the leading side of travel.

S2 makes the scientific invariant explicit:

```text
dot(tail_tip_rest - com_rest, forward_body) < 0
```

The forward axis points from the propulsive trailing edge toward the body. Donor `StepLive` remains an offline
conformance arm for force magnitudes, controller shapes, and transformed trace invariants, but its reversed
longitudinal convention cannot authorize S2. The independent analytic arm and the aft-tail invariant authorize
direction. No gain or compatibility switch exists in production.

### 2.3 Control versus dynamics

The physics input is a bounded `MotorCommand` containing gait effort and a DC turn-bias command. It changes the
joint trajectory; it never writes yaw, yaw rate, velocity, or position. A donor-shaped `HeadingController` in
`core` maps a desired ENU heading to the bounded turn bias using a derived authority cap, heading low-pass, and
slew limit. The controller is a placeholder policy and is reported as such.

The physical result is still surge plus yaw. S2 does not claim the future normalized yaw-rate action is validated.
That adapter must later be tested against the actual Talos/Sophia interface.

### 2.4 Energy language

S2 has two separate diagnostics:

- hydrodynamic channel identities, including reactive input power, wake power, fin loss, and drag dissipation;
- discrete linear and angular integrator work-consistency residuals.

Neither is called total-energy conservation. Sunlight, metabolism, reserve, heat, and fluid energy are not a
closed S2 system. A cumulative `mechanical_work_j` readout is derived/diagnostic and never a conserved reservoir.

## 3. Frozen contracts

### 3.1 Capacity and shape

```text
W          = number of worlds; authorizing S2 uses W=1
N_cap      = 5,120 or 10,240 for population gates
N_max      = 24 genotype nodes
E_max      = 48 genotype edges
S_max      = 16 developed physical segments
S_slot     = 17 including slot-0 sentinel

`N_max/E_max` are genotype capacities; `S_max` is the physical developed-body capacity. No document may call the
genotype capacity `S_max=32`. Over-budget expression is deterministically masked and surfaced in telemetry; it
never resizes tensors.

### 3.2 `GenotypeBatch`

Leading shape is `[W,N_cap,...]`. Runtime fields are SoA tensors:

edge_mask                 bool [W,N,E_max]

```

Innovation ids are present now so S3/S5 do not need a genome rewrite. S2 fixtures use valid rooted graphs with
one expressed root, acyclic non-self edges, and bounded terminal self-recursion. Mutation and registry ownership
arrive later.

Node slot 0 is the required root. Non-self edges may instantiate a node from more than one parent, but the node
graph must remain acyclic. For ordinary edges `edge_recursion=1`. For a self-edge, recursion `r` is the total run
length including the already-emitted node, so `r=3` adds two serial copies. Repeated instances also express the
node's ordinary outgoing edges, giving real repeated modules rather than a geometry-only chain.

Attachment is a fraction of the parent semi-axes: `local_pos = attach_frac * parent_axes`, with the lateral FLU-y
component multiplied by the instance side. Mirroring reflects across the FLU x-z sagittal plane using
`S=diag(1,-1,1)`: `R_mirror=S*R*S`. Edge scale multiplies the child node axes before its descendants are placed.

The donor's per-node `jFreq` and `jPhase` are deliberately not migrated: the live solver never reads them and uses
the body-level coherent `swim_freq/swim_wave` rhythm instead. Carrying dead heritable fields forward would recreate
the dual-genome failure in miniature. They can return only with a mechanism and an activation test.

### 3.3 Development contract

`develop(genotype) -> DevelopedBody` is a pure fixed-shape function. It performs a fixed **16-emission batched DFS
stack scan**, not a level-synchronous breadth-first walk. This distinction is load-bearing: the donor emits
`root, child, grandchild, sibling`, while a depth-layer scan would emit `root, child, sibling, grandchild`, changing
slot identity, cap truncation, tail tie-breaking, and float reduction order.

The stack has fixed width 16. Each of 16 unrolled emission iterations pops one instance per body, writes one unique
physical slot, constructs outgoing candidates in edge-slot order with positive side before the optional negative
mirror side, and prepends the candidates in reverse push order so the next pop preserves donor DFS. Each instance
carries node index, parent output slot, depth, side, transform, incoming edge, and remaining self-recursion count.
Stable integer prefix ranks perform bounded stack insertion; no `scatter_add_`, `index_add_`, atomics, boolean
shape compaction, Python per-creature loop, or host round-trip is allowed in the production path. Mirror and
recursion consume physical segment slots exactly as ordinary instances. Anything beyond the first 16 emissions is
masked and counted as overflow.

`DevelopedBody` is owned by `physics.contracts` and contains only derived physical geometry:

```text
seg_mask                  bool [W,N,S_slot]
parent                    i16  [W,N,S_slot]
depth                     i8   [W,N,S_slot]
local_pos_flu_m           f32  [W,N,S_slot,3]
local_rot_flu             f32  [W,N,S_slot,4]
semi_axes_flu_m           f32  [W,N,S_slot,3]
density_gene              f32  [W,N,S_slot]
mass_sim                  f32  [W,N,S_slot]
volume_m3                 f32  [W,N,S_slot]
drag_area_flu_m2          f32  [W,N,S_slot,3]
added_mass_flu_kg         f32  [W,N,S_slot,3]
fin_perpendicular_kg      f32  [W,N,S_slot]
is_surface, intake, sense bool [W,N,S_slot]
joint_amp_rad             f32  [W,N,S_slot]
hinge_axis_flu            f32  [W,N,S_slot,3]
phase_rad                 f32  [W,N,S_slot]
tail_slot                 i16  [W,N]
swim_freq_hz              f32  [W,N]
swim_wave_rad_per_depth   f32  [W,N]
truncated_candidate_count i16  [W,N]
```

Mass uses ellipsoid volume for both inertial and displaced geometry. `kg_per_sim_mass=250` is applied exactly once
when structural SI inertia is assembled. Added mass remains a separate Lamb term. Dead/sentinel rows are exact
zero except identity quaternions and sentinel-safe parent indices.

The three semi-axes are the only segment geometry for both Segment and Surface nodes. A Surface does not force a
hidden thickness or retain a separate donor `fin_span`: its local x-z plane is the lifting plane, local y is the
surface normal, chord is `2*axis_x`, span is `2*axis_z`, and thickness is `2*axis_y`. The Surface bit activates the
circulatory channel without reshaping the part. This makes Segment↔Surface a reversible one-bit exaptation rather
than a geometry rewrite.

`truncated_candidate_count` is the number of already-scheduled stack instances discarded by the 16-slot physical
budget or fixed stack bound; it is not mislabeled as the total size of an unexpanded infinite graph. Any nonzero
value is reported as cap pressure. Authorizing D0 conformance cases may exercise truncation intentionally; ordinary
H1/H2 bodies must report the frozen acceptable incidence in the fixture manifest.

`DevelopedBody` is never serialized as authoritative state. Snapshots store genotype plus live state and re-run
development on load; a test hard-diffs the reconstructed body.

The body object is immutable after development. Storing axes/density alongside their derived mass, areas, and
added mass is a compiled phenotype readout, not synchronized authority: there are no mutation methods or writers,
and every value is regenerated together from the genotype.

## 4. Physics contract

### 4.1 Pose and actuation

The fixed six-pass pose scan works in FLU. At each joint:

```text
theta_j(t) = effort * amp_j * sin(2*pi*freq*t + phase_j)
             + turn_bias * depth_j
```

`effort` is fixed at 1 in the authorizing donor-comparison corpus. The turn term is bounded so the maximum biased
joint excursion never exceeds the frozen 58-degree donor anchor. `turn_bias` changes pose only.

World pose is derived from ENU yaw. Linear motion remains horizontal: `velocity[...,2] == 0` and depth is unchanged
by physics. Core wrapping affects ENU x/y only.

### 4.2 Hydrodynamic contributor

The contributor retains the validated S0 channels with canonical axes:

- tail-tip gait velocity excludes rigid `omega x r` from reactive crossflow;
- reactive thrust and wake identity are evaluated at the aft tail tip;
- circulatory fin lift is additive and active only for a surface tail;
- axial form drag uses segment velocity including COM, rigid yaw, and gait components;
- `M_eff = M_body_kg*I + sum(R*diag(m_add)*R^T)` keeps structural and added mass separate;
- prescribed uniform fluid velocity enters world-position transport, not the relative-velocity force state;
- no vertical force is manufactured in S2.

The tail slot is the segment whose rest-pose local `-x` endpoint
`center + R*[-semi_axis_forward,0,0]` has the minimum FLU `x`; ties go to the later DFS slot. The sampled tail tip
is that same local `-x` endpoint at the current pose. This rule turns the donor's posterior `+z` chain into
canonical FLU aft `-x` and makes the tail-aft invariant executable rather than rhetorical.

The constrained linear solve is over ENU x/y. A dedicated `solve_constrained_xy` or axis-parameterized symmetric
solver must be used; reusing S0's x/z helper without an explicit frame adapter is forbidden.

### 4.3 Yaw dynamics

For segment moment arm `r_j` about the instantaneous body-frame COM:

```text
tau_hydro = (r_tail x F_thrust)_z + sum_j (r_j x F_drag_j)_z
C_yaw = 0.5 * rho * YawCd * sum_j(A_broadside_j * |r_j,planar|^3)
tau_drag = -C_yaw * omega * |omega|
L_1 = L_0 + (tau_hydro + tau_drag) * dt
omega_1 = L_1 / I_yaw,1
yaw_1 = wrap_pi(yaw_0 + omega_1 * dt)
```

`YawCd=1` is the broadside physical anchor, distinct from axial `Cd=0.1`. `C_yaw` may be rest-pose constant for
donor corroboration; the approximation is identified in telemetry. `I_yaw` includes structural SI point-mass and
added-mass terms. Its lower bound is a degenerate-input guard: any activation on a valid authorizing body is a hard
failure.

The 8 rad/s donor angular clamp is not used to make a run green. Production may retain an emergency assertion
threshold, but reaching it raises and records a falsifier rather than clipping state. Nonfinite state likewise
raises; it is never silently reset to zero or the last position.

### 4.4 Discrete work consistency

Linear residual retains S0's mixed, dimensioned formulation. Angular work uses the exact discrete identity for
changing yaw inertia:

```text
Krot_i = L_i^2 / (2*I_i)
Wimp_rot = 0.5*(omega_0 + omega_1)*(L_1 - L_0)
WdeltaI_rot = 0.5*L_0*L_1*(1/I_1 - 1/I_0)
Rrot = (Krot_1-Krot_0) - Wimp_rot - WdeltaI_rot
```

Per-step mixed tolerances are dimensioned in joules. Long-horizon authorization uses the existing prefix budget
form separately for translation and rotation. Monotone signed bias is reported. This validates the integrator; it
does not claim a closed universe energy balance.

### 4.5 Heading controller

The core controller follows the donor's useful shape:

- desired heading is normalized in ENU x/y;
- the first valid sample latches exactly; later samples use spherical/angular interpolation with alpha 0.35;
- heading error uses travel direction above 0.2 m/s and body heading below it;
- full turn authority occurs at 60 degrees of error;
- the command changes by at most 20% of per-body maximum authority per controller latch;
- the command cap is derived from actual joint amplitudes and depths.

Controller constants are policy anchors, not physical constants. They are frozen for S2 comparison and reported
separately. The physical yaw rate must remain drag-set.

## 6. Acceptance gates

Thresholds are frozen before authorizing measurements. A decision is `GO`, `CONDITIONAL GO`, or `NO-GO`; no
threshold is adjusted after observing a result.

### Gate A — authority and architecture

- active docs state ENU/FLU, tail-aft, energy scope, controller scope, and population floors consistently;
- import-linter enforces sibling packages and composition ownership;
- production modules are capability-named; no `s2` namespace;
- no live module imports oracle or fixture code.

### Gate B — development and representation

- the validation-only donor P0 compatibility readout, topology, transforms, clamps, and segment reconstruction
  match mixed tolerance `atol + rtol*|expected|`, with `rtol=1e-4` f64 and dimensioned absolute tolerances per
  field; final ellipsoid mass is explicitly excluded from the legacy box-mass equality;
- gain-1 transforms, volume, structural mass, and drag areas match at `rtol<=1e-9` f64; GL256 production Lamb
  added mass matches the independent GL512 arm at `rtol<=1e-7` (the frozen corpus maximum before production was
  `5.53e-8`);
- output shape is always `[W,N,17,...]` and slot-0 is sentinel-safe;
- DFS mirror/recursion/cap order matches the frozen literal slot contract;
- repeated development of the same genotype is exact on the same device;
- genotype perturbation changes the body; body mutation without genotype is structurally unavailable;
- snapshot restore re-develops the identical body;
- all authorizing bodies satisfy tail-aft and positive finite mass/inertia;
- zero runtime atomics, boolean compaction, or host per-creature loops in development.

### Gate C — live physical fidelity

- zero-turn straight cases agree with donor scalar channel magnitudes and gain-1 vector mechanics;
- positive and negative commands produce opposite, symmetric curvature beyond the zero-command drift band;
- a 90-degree desired heading homes and settles without runaway wrong-way rotation;
- a symmetric full-cycle gait has bounded yaw drift and nonzero forward displacement;
- yaw torque is the summed moment of forces; removing moment arms removes turning;
- quadratic yaw decay matches the analytic solution at `rtol<=1e-3` f32 and `1e-6` f64;
- linear and angular per-step mixed residuals pass; 100,000-step prefix budgets stay below `1e-3` f32 and
  `1e-6` f64 after step 100;
- signed bias is reported separately;
- solve regularization, inertia-floor activation, emergency angular threshold, and nonfinite repair counts are all
  exactly zero;
- no-light/no-field changes cannot alter mechanics; prescribed uniform fluid drift changes world-position
  transport but not stored relative velocity or hydrodynamic force in the pure transport fixture.

### Gate D — form is function

- AST/grep guard forbids `eff`, `swimProxy`, cached `speed`, `defense_score`, `agility_score`, and writeable
  capability arrays in production state;
- locomotion is measured from live displacement/work, never a genome scalar;
- feeding readiness is a stateless query from intake-port geometry; no S2 field biomass is debited;
- structural mass, displaced volume, projected areas, and inertia are derived from the same axes;
- paired morphology fixtures differ in both measured locomotion and intake geometry for an identified physical
  reason; the test does not require an arbitrary minimum ranking if the physics predicts otherwise;
- removing an intake port changes intake geometry but cannot change hydrodynamics unless that port's physical
  geometry also changes.

### Gate E — throughput and affordability

Run H1-live and H2-live at both population sizes on CPU and the pinned CUDA device, eager then compile; CUDA
graphs are attempted only if safe for mutable state. Five repetitions follow warmup. Report median, minimum, IQR,
peak allocation, compile time, and profiler attribution.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5,000 | 5,120 | 600,000 creature-steps/s | 3,000,000 | 6,000,000 |
| 10,000 | 10,240 | 1,200,000 creature-steps/s | 6,000,000 | 12,000,000 |

GO requires every H1/H2 authorization cell at 5,000 and 10,000 to clear the hard minimum using the minimum of five

## 10. Decision semantics

- **GO:** Gates A–F are green at both 5,000 and 10,000 on the declared viable device scope; zero authorizing
  intervention; both oracle arms green; S3 may proceed.
- **CONDITIONAL GO:** scientific and representation gates are green, but a named device/rung is excluded or a
  non-authorizing integration rehearsal misses. The condition is explicit and cannot hide a 10,000-body miss on
  every intended device.
- **NO-GO:** any frame/tail/body-authority violation, oracle failure, stat proxy, numerical intervention, physical
  drift failure, all-OOM measurement, or 10,000-body real-time failure on all intended devices.

The report preserves failed frozen thresholds. A later revision may establish a new authority, but it may not
rewrite an S2 NO-GO as if the failed experiment had passed.
